How to read

The recording tiles the three cameras as the models see them: top (left), wrist (top right), side (bottom right).

The right side shows the decision cycle behind the chunk being executed. Row 1 imagines the nominal chunk followed by each fallback; row 2 imagines each fallback alone. Each plan scores its least safe imagined step, J, circled on its margin trace; the grey line marks where the nominal chunk ends.

For the rollout filters, each decision cycle plays its imagination while the recording runs through the inference pause and then holds; the chunk then executes. Step 1 imagines the nominal chunk followed by each fallback; only if the chunk is rejected does step 2 imagine each fallback alone. On the bar, blue marks step 1, lavender step 2, grey an executed nominal chunk, and purple a fallback. Drag along the bar to scrub. Markers show every decision cycle, colored by its verdict: accepted, intervened, or rejected with no safer fallback; select one to jump to it.

Imagined plans are rendered by a decoder that no decision uses.

Definitions
Outcome
Success: the empty bottle is in the bin. Failure: the full bottle is knocked over. Timeout: the operator stops a trial that can no longer succeed, the arm stops on a fault, or the 20-chunk limit is reached.
Intervention
Over every decision cycle of success and timeout trials and those strictly before the fall in failure trials.
Filter compute
Mean ± SD of image encoding, prediction, classification, and calls to Learned or Q; excludes the nominal policy and the decoder.
Imagined steps
Each fallback enters twice: nominal then fallback (48 steps) and fallback alone (32 steps).
Training
RTX 5090 GPU-hours through the selected checkpoints. Base: dynamics model 11.67, failure classifier 0.01. Additional: decoder 1.00; training of Learned, which also yields Q, 0.17.

Training and filter construction

Data100 demonstrations, 191 policy executions
  • 100 teleoperated safe demonstrations, 19 min at 15 Hz; the nominal policy trains on 90 and validates on 10.
  • World model: raw sensor and actuator data traces from the 90 demonstrations and 191 nominal-policy executions (50,925 frames); 10 demonstrations and 25 executions validate, 25 more executions are a held-out test set.
  • 41% of the executions contain one exploration segment: 32 steps (2.1 s) of pink-noise motion around the last commanded target (35 mm, 5°, a gripper toggle in half), starting at a random chunk boundary among the first ten.
  • Observation: top, wrist, and side cameras (640 × 480) cropped and tiled into one 224 × 224 image, as in the recordings above.
  • Action: 10-D at 15 Hz (end-effector position, 6-D orientation, gripper), executed in 16-step chunks; the arm holds its last target while the next chunk is generated and checked.
Nominal policyCosmos 3 Edge, fine-tuned
  • Policy variant of Cosmos 3 Edge with new action heads; 3,000 iterations at batch 256 on 8 H100 GPUs (12.7 GPU-h).
  • Inputs: the composite image, end-effector pose and gripper state, task instruction. Outputs: 32 actions with frames; the first 16 are the proposed chunk.
World modelDINOv3 latents, transformer dynamics
  • Encoder: frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16; each image becomes 14 × 14 tokens of dimension 1024.
  • Dynamics model: 6 layers, 16 heads, causal over a 5-step latent context; each action is embedded and appended to every token; predicts the change from the last latent.
  • Training: latent mean squared error over 16-, 32-, and 48-step autoregressive predictions, discounted by 0.95 per step; 33,231 updates. Validation error 0.0094 versus 0.0196 for repeating the last latent.
  • Decoder: 4-layer transformer trained on pixel error; renders imagined plans for inspection only.
Failure classifiermargin on a 5-step latent window
  • 0.3 M-parameter convolutional head on the last 5 latent observations; the margin ℓ is the negative failure logit.
  • Binary cross-entropy on the labels below (failure from the onset on), input noise 0.05; per-frame AUROC 0.999 on physical validation frames.
  • Labels: Qwen3.8-27B, in two calls on top and side frame sheets, marks the first frame at which the full bottle lies flat.

FallbacksLeft, Right, Learned
  • Left, Right: 100 mm sideways in 16 steps, orientation and gripper held, then a 16-step hold.
  • Learned: 16 increments of at most 25 mm and 0.1 rad from the last target, gripper held, then a 16-step hold. Soft actor-critic in the world model's imagination on the discounted safety Bellman backup, with the failure classifier defining the avoid set; 256 parallel imagined episodes, selected after 100 training steps.
  • Critic Q: the minimum of the twin critics from the same training, on the nominal chunk followed by Learned.
Filteraccept, else intervene
  • Each plan scores its least safe imagined step: J(p) = minj ℓt+j.
  • Accept the nominal chunk if J, the best score over nominal-then-fallback plans, is ≥ 0; otherwise execute the best fallback-only plan if its score J is higher.
  • Value accepts when Q ≥ 0 and otherwise executes Learned.
  • Filter and nominal policy share one RTX 5090.
